# Commercial scope

Potential customers are independent specialty merchants, accessory sellers, office suppliers and hobby retailers with slow-moving stock. The implemented workflow explores feasible recovery offers while preserving explicit price and margin boundaries. Application-generated metrics measure configured inventory and recorded app events; there is no claim of market traction, customer validation, measured environmental savings or actual business revenue.

A future merchant SaaS could charge for the software, analytics or catalog connectors. Those subscriptions are not implemented. Ordinary PayPal checkout pays the configured sandbox merchant directly. There is no platform-owned escrow, automatic fee withholding or multi-merchant split. Live merchant onboarding and any platform payments product require suitable PayPal approval and regulatory review.

The user owns their provider account and controls model access, provider-side billing limits and retention. The app does not require funding a shared AI service. Local Ollama remains an optional zero-provider-key path.

Apache-2.0 permits commercial deployment, modification and SaaS operation subject to its terms. Others also retain rights under that license. Neither the repository name nor original artwork proves exclusive trademark rights. Production branding should undergo independent clearance.
